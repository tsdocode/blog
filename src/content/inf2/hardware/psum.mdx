PSUM (Partial Sum buffer) is where matmul results land. The <T id="hardware/tensor-engine">Tensor Engine</T>
cannot write to SBUF; every `nc_matmul` writes — or accumulates — fp32 values into PSUM, and the Vector or Scalar
Engine then copies the result back to SBUF.

## Accumulation groups

Each matmul either **overwrites** its bank or **accumulates** into it (`accumulate=True`). A chain of matmuls into
one bank — for example the 16 `K`-blocks of a 2048-wide contraction — is an accumulation group. With 8 banks the
Tensor Engine can have up to 8 groups in flight, so it starts the next block while the previous one is being
evicted.

## Who can touch it

- Tensor Engine: write and accumulate only.
- Vector and Scalar: read and write, but not at the same time.
- GpSimd: no access.

## Practical notes

- **Evict straight into the next op.** A residual add, bias add or activation can read PSUM directly, so the eviction
  copy and the next operation are one instruction.
- **Give each accumulation its own tile.** In our kernels, `accumulate=True` into a *slice* of a larger PSUM tile
  overwrote instead of accumulating; separate per-accumulation PSUM tiles fixed it.
- **Fill whole banks.** Four `[128, 128]` matmuls written into the four quarters of one `[128, 512]` bank can then
  be read back by a single Vector or Scalar instruction.
