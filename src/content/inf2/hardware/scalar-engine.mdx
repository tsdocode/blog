The Scalar Engine (the "activation" engine) handles operations where each output element depends on **one input
element**: activations, exponentials, square roots, scaling, casting, copies. It has 128 lanes at 1.4 GHz and
hardware implementations of the common non-linear functions.

## How it works

Every instruction computes `func(in * scale + bias)` per element, where `scale` and `bias` can be per-partition
values. The multiply-add is pipelined — it costs nothing extra. On NeuronCore-v2 an instruction can also keep a
**running sum** of its outputs per partition, so a softmax's `exp` and the row sum of the exponentials come out of
the same instruction.

Things to know:

- It reads **one** tensor per instruction. Anything that combines two tiles goes to the
  <T id="hardware/vector-engine">Vector Engine</T>.
- `sin` is only accurate on `[-π, π]`; larger arguments need range reduction first (see
  <T id="patterns/sin-range-reduction">sin range reduction</T>).
- The "copy" activation can read PSUM, which makes ScalarE a second engine for evicting matmul results when the
  Vector Engine is busy.
