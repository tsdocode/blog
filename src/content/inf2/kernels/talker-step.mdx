Qwen3-TTS generates speech in three stages per 80 ms audio frame. The first is the **talker**: a 28-layer,
1.4 B-parameter transformer (hidden 2048, 16 query / 8 KV heads of 128, MLP 6144) that predicts the first codebook
token of the next frame. In a serving engine with 32 streams per core, one talker step is one decode token per
stream — plus any new prompts that arrive.

## One launch, mixed batch

The kernel takes all of this step's tokens as one transposed activation `xT [2048, T]`:

- **32 decode rows**, one per slot (empty slots are padding), attending to their paged KV cache —
  <T id="kernels/paged-kv-attention">paged KV attention</T>.
- **0–3 tiles of 128 packed prompt tokens**, attending within their own prompt —
  <T id="kernels/packed-prefill">varlen packed prefill</T>.

Every layer runs RMSNorm → fused QKV projection → per-head norm + RoPE (<T id="patterns/qk-norm-rope">as matmuls</T>)
→ KV-cache write → attention for each token group → output projection → residual → RMSNorm → fused SwiGLU MLP →
residual. Activations stay in SBUF across all 28 layers; the 2.82 GB of weights stream through, block by block.

## Where the time goes

| Part | Time (32 decode streams) | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Dense: projections + MLP | 11.0 ms | 2.82 GB at 247 GB/s — at the HBM ceiling |
| Attention + KV write | ~8 ms | many tiny matmuls; ~3× its byte floor |
| + 3 tiles of new prompts | +20 ms | compute-bound: ~1.1 TFLOP of prefill |

The dense part is done: it is limited by HBM bandwidth, and only fewer weight bytes (FP8) would make it faster. The
attention is the remaining inefficiency — see <T id="performance/matmul-cost">the matmul cost model</T>.

## Design choices that mattered

- **Weights as the stationary operand, tokens moving.** With ≤ 512 tokens, every weight tile is loaded once per
  step and all tokens stream through it.
- **Token-chunked linears.** Above 512 tokens a moving tile no longer fits one PSUM bank, and the activations no longer
  fit SBUF with everything else. Linears and SwiGLU process tokens in chunks of 512 — but keep a whole step at or
  below 512 tokens anyway, or every weight block runs a second, short matmul (see
  <T id="patterns/static-shape-buckets">buckets</T>).
- **SwiGLU straight out of PSUM.** Gate and up are interleaved in the weight tiling so `silu(gate) · up` is computed
  during the eviction; the 12,288-wide intermediate is never materialised.
