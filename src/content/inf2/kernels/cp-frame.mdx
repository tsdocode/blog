After the talker picks codebook 0, Qwen3-TTS's **code predictor** fills in the other 15 codebooks of the frame, one
after another: a small 5-layer transformer (hidden 1024) runs 16 depth steps per frame, and each step's sampled
token selects the next step's input embedding. That is a strictly sequential loop — and on the host, every step was a
kernel launch, a sampler launch and a round trip.

## Everything stays on chip

One `cp_frame` launch runs, for all 32 streams:

1. **16 depth steps of the 5-layer transformer**, with its tiny KV cache (≤ 16 slots) written in place. Attention over
   ≤ 16 slots uses element-wise `q ⊙ K` and a ones-matmul instead of per-row matmuls; the per-step RoPE and norm
   weight are folded into one matrix per step (see <T id="patterns/qk-norm-rope">QK-norm and RoPE</T>).
2. **The vocabulary head with streams on partitions.** Swapping matmul operands produces logits as
   `[streams, 2048]`, so each stream's vocabulary is on the free axis.
3. **Top-50 sampling per stream:**
   - 7 rounds of `max8` (the 8 largest values per partition) plus `nc_find_index8` (their positions), with
     `nc_match_replace8` knocking out each round's winners — 56 sorted candidates, the first 50 used;
   - `exp((v − v_max) · 1/T)`, then the CDF as a transpose plus a matmul with a triangular matrix of ones;
   - inverse-CDF sampling with host-supplied uniforms: count the CDF entries below `u · total`, then a one-hot select
     of the token index.
4. **The next input.** The sampled token ids are converted to int32 and used as a `vector_offset` gather of the
   projected embedding rows (see <T id="programming/indirect-dma">indirect DMA</T>), transposed on the Tensor Engine
   back to `[1024, streams]`.

Only the 15 sampled tokens per stream go back to the host.

## Same algorithm, same tokens

The sampler follows exactly the reference algorithm (top-k with per-stream temperature, inverse CDF with the same
uniforms, argmax for greedy streams), and against the per-step path with the same random numbers the tokens agree
100%, greedy and sampled.

## Gen2 surprises

Two "obvious" instructions failed to compile on NeuronCore-v2:

- `nc_match_replace8` **with** its `dst_idx` output is unimplemented → indices come from `nc_find_index8` on an
  untouched copy of the logits.
- `tensor_scalar_cumulative` (a running sum) compiled on its own but failed to lower in this kernel → the CDF became
  two small matmuls.

See <T id="performance/gen2-gotchas">gen2 gotchas</T> for the full list.
